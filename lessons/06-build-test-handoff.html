Your project brief

Seven staff: five wired desktops, two Wi-Fi laptops, one wired printer, one AP, and a router uplink. Add two future wired devices. Staff need printing and internet access; visitors need internet only.

Your submission must include requirements, topology and capacity, addressing, access policy, evidence, and ownership/recovery. Keep real passwords out of the document.
